What is Marriage Biodata?

Marriage Biodata is a professional profile used during marriage proposals. It contains important personal details such as name, age, education, occupation, family information, horoscope details, contact information, and photograph in a clean and attractive format.

Why is it Used?

  • ✔ Share complete personal information in one page.

  • ✔ Easy to print, download and share on WhatsApp or Email.
